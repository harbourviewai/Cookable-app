04 Construction Geometry, ratios, spec
Canvas 1024 × 1024 master, square, no transparency. Every export rasters from this single SVG.
Safe area 10% margin on all four sides (102px). Bowl + swirl never touch this boundary, so iOS, Android, circle, and square masks all crop cleanly.
Bowl proportions Width 580px (56% of canvas). Rim sits at y=560 (~55% down); bottom curve at y=744 (~73%). Centered horizontally on x=512.
Bowl construction Linen body, single closed path. Rim opening is a Pine-Light ellipse (rx=278, ry=34) — the only depth cue. No highlights, no gradients, no skeuomorphic rims.
Steam swirl Single continuous saffron stroke. Width 56px (5.5% of canvas), round caps, round joins. Two loose S-turns rising from y=504 to y=40. Hand-confident, never wobbly.
Color discipline Three fills total — Pine, Linen, Saffron — plus Pine-Light for inner shadow. No gradients, no noise, no shadow under the icon.
