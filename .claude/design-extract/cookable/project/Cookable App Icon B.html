04 Construction Geometry, ratios, spec
Canvas1024 × 1024 master, square, no transparency. Single SVG, all variants by token swap.
Safe area12% margin (123px) on all four sides. Plate floats in the Pine field with breathing room — every platform mask crops cleanly.
PlateLinen circle, radius 343 (~67% of canvas). Centered on (512, 512). Inner well ring at radius 291 (~85% of plate diameter), 6px Pine-Light stroke at 0.85 opacity — the only depth cue.
Sprig axisGroup rotated −22° around the plate's lower-third pivot at (512, 560). Sits just below true center so the negative space above feels intentional.
StemSingle saffron path, 34px stroke (~3.3% of canvas), rounded caps. Slight bow — confident, not ruler-straight. Spans ~340px (~50% of plate diameter).
LeavesThree small filled leaf shapes alternating sides — back-left, upper-mid, lower-right. Each drawn as a single closed bezier; no veins, no outlines. Hand-confident silhouette over botanical accuracy.
Color disciplineThree fills total — Pine, Linen, Saffron — plus Pine-Light for the inner ring. No gradients, no shadow under the plate, no realism.
