## Voice rules to apply when adding new strings

- Sentence case
- Contractions OK
- No exclamation points except `Saved! ✓` style and recipe encouragement
- **Banned:** amazing, delicious, yummy, scrumptious, "powered by AI", "AI-powered"
- Use "you" liberally
- Direct + warm, not saccharine
